## Introduction
The budgeting function employs the same concepts as GnuCash and is compatible with it. 
Please refer to the relevant official GnuCash documentation: 
1. [Zero-Based Budgeting](https://wiki.gnucash.org/wiki/Zero-Based_Budgeting)
2. [Chapter 14. Budgets](https://www.gnucash.org/docs/v5/C/gnucash-guide/chapter_budgets.html)

## How to delete, and modify a budget?
Slide your finger left on a budget, which reveals the actions you can do on that budget. See the figure below.
You can google the [swipe gesture](https://www.google.com/search?q=ios+gesture+swipe+list+item) if you are not familiar with this operation.
